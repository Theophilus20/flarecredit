Score transport Local data

Your binding preimage, collected proofs, and activity feed are stored privately in this browser and never leave it except to the enclave. Your score and identity link live on-chain and persist across devices.

Backup & recovery

Your score, identity link, and lending position live on-chain and always survive. But your binding preimage and collected proofs are stored only in this browser clearing site data or switching devices loses them, and old XRPL transactions can fall outside the verifier's history window, making them impossible to re-attest. Export a backup file to keep them safe.

The file contains your XRPL address, nonce, and Merkle proofs the same data the enclave sees. Keep it private; anyone holding it could recompute a score for your Flare address.
