What is FlareCredit?

DeFi lending treats everyone as an anonymous stranger, so every loan is over-collateralized typically 150% or more. Meanwhile millions of XRP holders have years of genuine payment history that would prove they're low-risk. FlareCredit closes that gap: you prove your XRPL history is real and yours, privately, and receive a signed credit score that lending markets can act on. Better score, less collateral, same wallet and nothing about your finances becomes public.

How it works

Four steps. Your XRPL address and transactions never touch the public chain.

1

Link your identity

Sign a one-time challenge with your XRPL wallet to prove ownership. Only keccak(flare, xrpl, nonce) is stored on-chain never the address itself.

2

Attest your history

Flare's Data Connector independently verifies your XRPL payments and issues Merkle proofs, which are sent straight to the enclave not to a public contract.

3

Score inside a TEE

A confidential enclave checks the proofs against on-chain Merkle roots, computes your 0–1000 score, and signs it with an attested key. Only the number leaves.

4

Borrow for less

The FXRP lending pool reads your score on-chain. Reach 700 and your collateral requirement drops from 150% to 120% roughly 25% more borrowing power.

Only possible on Flare

FlareCredit uses all four of Flare's enshrined protocols. No bridges, no external oracles, no trusted third parties.

FDC

Flare Data Connector

Proves your XRPL Payment transactions actually happened, with Merkle proofs anchored to on-chain roots the verified source of your credit history.

FCC

Confidential Computing

Runs the scoring enclave in a Trusted Execution Environment. Scores are only accepted from a whitelisted code hash trust the code, not the operator.

FTSO

Time Series Oracle

Decentralized FLR/USD and XRP/USD feeds price your collateral and debt, so borrowing power and liquidations stay accurate in real time.

FAssets

FXRP

Brings XRP onto Flare as usable DeFi collateral, turning your credit score into an actual lending product rather than a standalone number.

Private by construction

Privacy here isn't a policy promise it's what the architecture allows to exist on-chain.

Hashed identity

The XRPL↔Flare link is stored as a hash. The preimage goes only to the enclave, so no observer can map your wallets.

Proofs stay off-chain

Merkle proofs of your payments are handed to the TEE, never verified publicly. Only roots and your score exist on-chain.

Attested scoring

Reproducible builds mean anyone can verify which code produced your score and the registry rejects any other code hash.
