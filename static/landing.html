What is FlareCredit?

In DeFi, everyone is an anonymous stranger so every loan demands more collateral than it's worth. Yet millions of XRP holders sit on years of real financial history. FlareCredit lets you prove that history is yours and that it's real, privately, and turn it into a credit score that lenders can trust so you borrow more with less locked up.

How it works

Four steps. Your wallet stays private the whole way.

1

Link your identity

Sign a challenge with your XRPL wallet to prove ownership. Only a hash of the link goes on-chain never your address.

2

Attest your history

Flare's Data Connector proves your XRPL payments really happened, with Merkle proofs sent privately to the enclave.

3

Score in a TEE

A confidential enclave verifies the proofs and computes your score, then signs it. The chain only ever sees the number.

4

Borrow for less

Your score gates the lending pool: reach 700 and your collateral drops from 150% to 120% same wallet, more borrowing power.
