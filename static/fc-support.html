Common questions

My attestation is taking a long time

FDC voting rounds take two to three minutes to finalise, and the proof only becomes available afterwards. The status line updates on every check. If it passes four minutes, the round was likely missed request the attestation again.

Why was my proof rejected?

Most often the payment wasn't sent from your linked XRPL address the enclave only counts your own history. Duplicates, self-payments, and failed XRPL transactions are also discarded. The exact reason appears beside your score.

My score won't reach 700

Attest more payments, ideally larger ones to different counterparties. Volume is capped per counterparty to prevent farming, so a spread of genuine transactions scores higher than repeated transfers to a single address.

Can I use FlareCredit on mainnet?

Not yet it runs on Coston2 testnet while the production confidential enclave and audit are completed. Follow us on X or Discord for the mainnet announcement.

How do I report a security issue?

Email flarecreditco@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject, or pick Security disclosure in the form. We acknowledge quickly and will work with you on responsible disclosure.
