How it works

Four simple steps. Complete privacy

Every step can be verified, while your XRPL address and payment history remain private.

01

Link your identity

Off-chain signature · on-chain hash

Sign a one-time challenge with your XRPL wallet. Only keccak(flare, xrpl, nonce) is stored on-chain the addresses themselves never appear, so no observer can map your wallets together.

02

Attest your history

Flare Data Connector

Around a hundred independent data providers verify your XRPL payments against their own nodes and commit a Merkle root on-chain. You receive proofs which go straight to the enclave, never to a public verifier.

03

Score inside a TEE

Confidential Computing

A confidential enclave checks each proof against the on-chain root, confirms the history is yours, computes a score from 0 to 1000, and signs it with an attested key tied to a published code hash.

04

Borrow for less

FTSOv2 · FAssets FXRP

The lending pool reads your score directly from the registry. Cross 700 and your collateral requirement drops from 150% to 120% roughly 25% more borrowing power on the exact same deposit.

Privacy model

Privacy built into the system

FlareCredit keeps your financial data private at every step. Here's exactly what each party can and cannot access.

PartyCan seeCannot see
Public chain Binding hash, score, Merkle roots XRPL address, transactions, wallet mapping
Lending pool Your score and its expiry Any detail of the history behind it
Other users That some address holds a score Which XRPL wallet it belongs to
The enclave Everything needed to compute a score Nothing but its exact code is public and attested

Why that last row matters: The enclave can process your data, but it cannot secretly run different software. Anyone can verify the code that generated your credit score, and any other code is rejected.
