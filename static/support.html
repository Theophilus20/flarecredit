Common questions

Quick answers to what people ask most.

My attestation is taking a long time

FDC voting rounds take roughly two to three minutes to finalise, and the proof only becomes available afterwards. The status message updates on every check. If it passes about four minutes, the round may have been missed try attesting again.

My proof was rejected why?

The most common reason is that the payment wasn't sent from your linked XRPL address; the enclave only counts your own history. Duplicates, self-payments, and failed XRPL transactions are also discarded. The exact reason appears beside your score result.

My score didn't reach 700

Attest more payments, ideally larger ones to different counterparties. Volume is capped per counterparty to prevent farming, so a spread of genuine transactions scores higher than repeated transfers to one address.

Can I use FlareCredit on mainnet?

Not yet FlareCredit runs on Coston2 testnet while the production confidential enclave and audit are completed. Follow us on X or Discord for the mainnet announcement.

How do I report a security issue?

Please email flarecreditco@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject, or choose Security disclosure in the form above. We'll acknowledge quickly and work with you on responsible disclosure.
